What Omakase Means お任せ

Trust, Handed to the Chef

Omakase — “I leave it to you.” A Japanese tradition where the chef decides, course by course, and the guest simply trusts the hand behind the counter.

The YAO way isn’t a purist’s omakase. It’s Japanese cuisine reimagined through the terroir and the sea of Baja — contemporary, chef-driven, built on the same trust and precision as tradition, but never bound by it. Expect the unexpected: dishes built for this coast, not copied from Tokyo. We honor the discipline. We take it further.
